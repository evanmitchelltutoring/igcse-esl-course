A report for the council

A popular walking route near your town has become dangerous in bad weather, and there have been several recent rescues. Your local council has asked young people who use the route to report on the problem. Write a report of 150–200 words.

A model · learn the shape, don't copy it

"Report on the Safety of the Ridgeway Path"

Introduction. This report describes the dangers of the popular Ridgeway walking route and recommends improvements, following several recent rescues.

The route and its popularity. The Ridgeway is a five-mile path along the hills above the town. It offers fine views and is used by hundreds of walkers every weekend, from families to serious hikers.

The dangers. The chief problem is the weather. In clear conditions the path is straightforward, but mist can descend within minutes, and walkers quickly lose the trail. The upper section is badly exposed to wind, and the ground becomes treacherous after rain. Many who get into difficulty are inexperienced and poorly equipped.

Recommendations. I would recommend three measures. First, the council should install clear marker posts, so the path can be followed even in mist. Second, a warning board could remind walkers to check the forecast. Third, a simple shelter near the summit would give stranded walkers somewhere safe to wait.

Notice → headings, neutral voice, passive ("is used by / is exposed"), and each recommendation paired with a reason ("so…").
