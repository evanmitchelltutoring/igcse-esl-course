The idea · copy this into your notes

Three things clothes say

We can't switch the message off. Whatever you wear signals something — the only choice is what. All unit we'll ask which of these three a piece of clothing is doing.
Belonging
"I'm one of you" — a group, a team, a place
Individuality
"I'm me" — what makes you different
Values
"This matters to me" — money, ethics, culture

Answer · mark your own

Which phrase fits?

1. A school uniform says something about belonging to one community.
2. Online, there's pressure to own the latest trainers.
3. There's a fine line between standing out and trying too hard.
4. Do you dress up or down? Honestly, it depends on the situation.
Watch me first · a model answer

Do I dress to fit in or stand out?

For me, it depends on the situation. At school there's pressure to look like everyone else, but on weekends what matters to me is comfort, because I want my clothes to say I'm relaxed and myself.
