The listening toolkit · copy this

Four moves for every listening part

1 · Read first
Use the seconds before the clip to read the question and options.
2 · Predict
Guess the type of answer — a time? a place? a reason?
3 · Meaning, not words
The answer is usually reworded — listen for what it means.
4 · Second play = check
Answer on the first play; use the second only to confirm.
listen
Station 1 · Part 1 · short recordings

Five clips — choose A, B or C

Each clip is read twice. Read the options before each one.

1 · Airport announcement — the new departure time is:   A 14:14  ·  B 14:40  ·  C gate 22
2 · Homestay host — the one rule of the house is:   A dinner at seven  ·  B shoes off at the door  ·  C no fruit
3 · Tour guide — inside the temple, visitors must:   A take photos  ·  B put cameras away  ·  C stay outside
4 · Host parent — what impressed her about the guest:   A she stayed three weeks  ·  B she settled in fast  ·  C she likes bread
5 · Traveller — getting lost meant he:   A wasted the day  ·  B saw more of the city  ·  C lost his map
Predict before you listen: clip 1 wants a time; clips 4 & 5 want a reason. That's move 2.
Answer · mark your own

Station 1 — the answers

1 — B. "your new departure time is 14:40, not 14:14 as shown earlier."
2 — B. "The only rule is shoes off at the door."
3 — B. "inside the temple please put your cameras away."
4 — B. "three weeks and she already orders her own bread… that took our last guest months."
5 — B. "I saw more of the city that way."

Station 2 · Part 3 · gapped notes

"Understanding culture shock" — one or two words per gap

The talk is read twice. Before you listen, predict: is each gap a feeling? a stage name?

Stage 1: the ______ stage — everything feels exciting and new
Stage 2: novelty fades → small differences cause ______
Stage 3: gradual ______ — you learn the unwritten rules
Stage 4: the new culture feels ______
Key message: culture shock is a sign of ______, not failure
Two tips: keep a ______; build a daily ______
Answer · mark your own

Station 2 — the notes filled

Stage 1: the honeymoon stage
Stage 2: small differences cause frustration
Stage 3: gradual adjustment
Stage 4: the culture feels normal
Key message: a sign of learning
Two tips: keep a diary; build a daily routine

Station 3 · Part 4 · six speakers, match A–H

Two statements are extra

The statements
A · learned to enjoy not understanding everything
B · came back with a new daily habit
C · value my own country more now
D · made a friend I still keep in touch with
E · realised how little I actually need
F · kept a language I still use
G · stopped judging places so quickly
H · missed the food more than the people
Speaker 1 · gave away half his clothes__
Speaker 2 · makes tea like her host gran__
Speaker 3 · still texts a friend__
Speaker 4 · no longer thinks one country is "right"__
Speaker 5 · comfortable not knowing what's going on__
Speaker 6 · kept enough to order food & joke__
Cross off each letter as you use it — two are left over.
Answer · mark your own

Station 3 — the answers

Speaker 1 — E. "a backpack for a year taught me I was carrying too much."
Speaker 2 — B. "Every morning now I make tea the way my host grandmother did."
Speaker 3 — D. "we still text across three time zones."
Speaker 4 — G. "I used to think one country had it right… I do not think that any more."
Speaker 5 — A. "oddly comfortable not knowing exactly what is going on."
Speaker 6 — F. "I kept enough to order food and make people laugh. I still use it."

Not needed: C (value own country) and H (missed the food).
