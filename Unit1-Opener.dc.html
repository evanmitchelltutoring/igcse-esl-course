The idea behind the unit

What is a third place?

First place
Home
Where you live. Given to you.
Second place
School / Work
Where you must be. Given to you.
Third place
Where you belong
A public place — not home, not school — that you choose, where you feel accepted.

This unit visits four of them — and asks what happens to a city when its third places disappear.

Where the unit takes you

Four third places

01
Study Cafés
Pay by the hour, not the cup. Quiet, warm, nobody asks you to leave. Reading for facts & feeling.
02
The 24-Hour Konbini
A convenience store that never closes — cheap, open, no pressure to spend. Vocabulary & listening.
03
Indoor Worlds
The mall as free, cool, social space — three ordered reasons why. Reading & summarising.
04
The Disappearing Hangout
When rents rise, the cheap in-between places vanish — one closed shop at a time. Inference & writer's attitude.

How we annotate every text

Three colours, three questions

Every time we read closely, we mark in these three colours.

Teal
Meaning
What does the text say? The facts, the point, the plain sense.
Terracotta
Method
How does the writer say it? The word chosen, the image, the effect.
Plum
Response
How does it make the reader feel or react? Your honest reaction.

Where the unit is heading

Capstone: My Third Place

In your group, choose a real place where people gather. Describe it, review it, and argue why it should be protected. Present to the class.

Describe
Bring the place alive — sights, sounds, the feeling of being there.
Review
Give an honest verdict — what works, what doesn't, who it's for.
Argue
Make the case: why does this place matter, and who would miss it?
