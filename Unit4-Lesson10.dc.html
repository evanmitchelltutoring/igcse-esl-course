The task · read it twice
A student magazine runs a series, Lessons from Away. Write a 150–200 word article about a time you — or someone you know well — lived in or stayed a long while in another place, and what it taught you. Use the present perfect; shape it Experience → Reflection → Resonance.
Cover all three — or lose content marks
Experience
one time you lived in / stayed somewhere different
Reflection
what changed, or what you noticed, while there
Resonance
what it taught you & why it stays with you
Register: warm, first-person, organised. Use 5+ of: belonging · perspective · adapt · routine · nostalgia · home.
Weak → strong · lift your writing

  • Only events, no reflection or feeling
  • Past simple throughout — no present perfect

A model article

Read it · then write your own

Lessons from a Year Away

I have lived in two places I would call home, but it is the second that changed me. For one long year, I stayed with a host family far from everything familiar, and at first I felt completely lost.

What surprised me was how much the small things mattered. The food was different, the streets were louder, and even the way people greeted each other took time to learn. Slowly, I began to adapt. I built a new routine — the same bus, the same bakery, a few friendly faces — and the strange place started to feel like mine.

It taught me that belonging is something you build, not something you are simply given. I came home with a wider perspective and a soft nostalgia for a street I may never walk again. If I have learned one thing, it is that home can be more than one place.

Experience — scene set
Reflection — what changed
Resonance — what it taught
