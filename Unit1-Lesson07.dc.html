Grammar · saying what you like more

Three preference patterns

enjoy / like / love + -ing
I enjoy studying there.  ✗ I enjoy to study.
prefer + X + to + Y
I prefer the library to the café.  ✗ prefer … than …
would rather + bare verb (+ than)
I'd rather sit in the corner than by the door.  ✗ would rather to sit
Watch me build one

Habit + preference, in one

HABIT — I often study at the café after school.
+ PREFERENCE — I prefer it to the library, and I'd rather sit near the window.

Corrected

1. I usually go to the café after school.
2. She goes there every weekend.
3. We prefer to meet / meeting at the store.
4. Do you like to study / studying here?
5. They are never late. (drop the clashing “usually”)
