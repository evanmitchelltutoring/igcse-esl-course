The skill · how to learn a word

Never learn a word alone

A word you learn on its own is easy to forget. Learn it with its family and one partner word it likes to sit beside — and it sticks.
Word family
One idea, many forms:
endure → endurance → enduring.
Knowing the family means you always have the right form.
Collocation (partner word)
Words that go together sound natural:
treacherous conditions · ration the water · lose your composure.

Eight words, each with a partner

treacherous (adj)
dangerous, esp. when it looks safe · treacherous conditions
stranded (adj)
left with no way to leave · stranded on the ridge
composure (noun)
calm self-control · keep your composure
endure (verb)
suffer something patiently · endure the cold
ration (verb)
limit use so it lasts · ration the water
summit (noun)
the highest point · reach the summit
signal (verb)
send a call for help · signal for help
persevere (verb)
keep trying despite difficulty · persevere to the end
