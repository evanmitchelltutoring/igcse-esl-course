The rule · copy this into your notes

Happening now, changing now

Form
subject + is / are + verb-ing
She is wearing vintage. They are buying less.
Growing trend
more and more + noun · is becoming more and more + adj
More and more people are thrifting.
Watch the spelling: change → changing (drop the e) · run → running (double the consonant) · use → using.
Never drop is/are ("She wearing"); never leave the base verb ("is change").

Answer · mark your own

Find and fix

1. More people are wearing second-hand clothes. (plural → are)
2. The style is changing quickly this year. (is + verb-ing)
3. More and more teenagers are wearing bright colours. (plural → are)
4. Sneakers are becoming more and more popular. (word order)
5. She is wearing a vintage jacket today. (base verb → -ing)
6. Designers are using more recycled fabric. (base verb → -ing)
