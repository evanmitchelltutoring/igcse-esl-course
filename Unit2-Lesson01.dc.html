The idea · copy this into your notes

Habitat and ecosystem

habitat
the natural home of an animal or plant.
ecosystem
a community of living things together with the environment they depend on.
The big idea
Every plant and animal lives in a habitat — and when habitats are damaged, whole ecosystems can collapse. Living things and their environment depend on each other.

Six words for the natural world

habitat
/ˈhæb·ɪ·tæt/ · noun
the natural home of an animal or plant
ecosystem
/ˈiː·kəʊ·sɪs·təm/ · noun

species
/ˈspiː·ʃiːz/ · noun
a group of living things that can breed together
adapt
/əˈdæpt/ · verb
to change in order to survive better
thrive
/θraɪv/ · verb
to grow strongly and do very well
fragile
/ˈfrædʒ·aɪl/ · adjective

Which one is a habitat?

1A coral reef where fish feed and shelter
2A documentary about the ocean
3A bottle of water on a shelf
4A drawing of a forest
Answer

Number 1 — the coral reef

A habitat is a real, natural home where living things live and depend on their surroundings.
The others are about nature — a film, a product, a drawing — but none is a place where living things actually live.
