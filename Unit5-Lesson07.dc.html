The rule · copy this into your notes

A ladder of certainty

must
I'm sure it's true.
"It must be AI."
might / could
It's possible.
"It might be a photo."
can't
I'm sure it's false.
"A human can't have done it."
Form: modal + be for now — "It must be AI."  ·  modal + have been for the past — "It must have been made by AI."
Never to after the modal; never an -s (not "musts").

Practice 1 · spot the error

Find and fix one mistake in each

1. This painting must to be very old.
2. That can't been made by a computer.
3. The artist might is famous.
4. It musts be a photograph.
5. She could to have painted it.
6. It must has been made by a computer.
Support → check each against the rule: no to, no -s, and use have been for the past.
Stretch → which two sentences are about the past? How do you know?
Answer · mark your own

Find and fix

1. This painting must be very old. (no to after must)
2. That can't have been made by a computer. (can't have + past participle)
3. The artist might be famous. (modal + base be)
4. It must be a photograph. (modals take no -s)
5. She could have painted it. (no to after could)
6. It must have been made by a computer. (modal + have + past participle)

Practice 2 · transform

Rewrite using the modal in brackets

1. "I'm sure this is AI art." (must)
2. "Perhaps the artist used a computer." (might)
3. "It's impossible that a human painted this so fast." (can't)
4. "Maybe it's a real photo." (could)
5. "I'm certain she didn't draw this by hand." (can't)
6. "Perhaps the machine made a mistake." (might)
Support → "so fast" and "didn't draw" are past — use have + past participle.
Stretch → rewrite one more of your own from a picture in the room.
Answer · mark your own

Rewrite

1. This must be AI art.
2. The artist might have used a computer.
3. A human can't have painted this so fast.
4. It could be a real photo.
5. She can't have drawn this by hand.
6. The machine might have made a mistake.

Gap-fill

…the artist must (1) have great talent. A small child can't (2) have painted it. A computer might / could (3) have helped. It can't (4) be an old painting. The museum might / could (5) show more works like this soon.
