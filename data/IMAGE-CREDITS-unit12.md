## Notes
- **Scope:** Unit 12 is now imaged in full — **Opener + Lessons 1–12** (L8–L12 were built by the Design chat mid-session and imaged in a second pass). The `Unit12-Exam-Inclass/Takehome` and Student/Teacher packet decks carry no hero image slots (consistent with every other unit).
- **Dedup across the unit's many "wilderness" slots:** opener-hero = blue misty peaks; l1 = a snow boot-print; l3 = a frosty forest stream (sunrise); l5 = a pink polar plain (sledge). Each is a distinct treatment so no two read alike; l6 uses a tent-interior refuge framing to stay unambiguously face-free.
- **l2 was re-sourced during selection:** the initial "hiking essentials" flat-lay was saturated with brand logos (GoPro, Bushnell, REI, Samsung, Banana Boat) and the alternative was branded bushcraft blades — both rejected in favour of a clean, brand-free compass-on-a-map (which also better fits the "Explorer & Frontier" vocabulary strand).
