# Extra grammar practice — drill banks (Units 1–2)

Drop-in extra practice for the grammar lessons: each set has **Spot-the-Error (6)**, **Word-Order / Transform (6)**, a **Gap-Fill in context** (themed to the unit), and a **Build-Your-Own** (framed). Use as extra deck slides (Q then keyed A) or as a worksheet page in the packet. **Answer keys are TEACHER-ONLY.** All items reuse the unit's own vocabulary and theme so grammar and content reinforce each other.

---

## UNIT 1 · L4 — Describing a Place (adjective order · *there is/are* · prepositions of place)

**A. Spot the error** (find and fix one mistake in each)
1. There is two comfortable chairs by the window. → *There **are** two comfortable chairs by the window.*
2. It's a wooden small table. → *It's a **small wooden** table.* (size before material)
3. The café is in the corner of the street. → *The café is **on** the corner of the street.*
4. There are a quiet room at the back. → *There **is** a quiet room at the back.*
5. I sat on a red big sofa. → *I sat on a **big red** sofa.* (size before colour)
6. The lamp hangs in the ceiling. → *The lamp hangs **from** the ceiling.*

**B. Put the adjectives in order**
1. wooden / small / a / table → *a small wooden table*
2. red / big / a / sofa → *a big red sofa*
3. old / lovely / a / building → *a lovely old building* (opinion before age)
4. round / white / a / clock → *a round white clock* (shape before colour)
5. Japanese / modern / a / café → *a modern Japanese café* (age before origin)
6. cotton / soft / a / cushion → *a soft cotton cushion*

**C. Gap-fill** — complete with *is / are* or a preposition (*on, in, by, from*)
"In my favourite café, there __(1)__ a long counter __(2)__ the window. __(3)__ the corner there __(4)__ two soft armchairs. __(5)__ the wall there are old photographs, and a warm lamp hangs __(6)__ the ceiling."
**Key:** 1 is · 2 by · 3 In · 4 are · 5 On · 6 from

**D. Build your own** (frames): *In my favourite place, there is ___ . / There are ___ . / In the corner, ___ . / On the wall, ___ .* — use **two adjectives in the correct order** at least once.

## UNIT 1 · L7 — Habits & Preferences (present simple · adverbs of frequency · *like/prefer* + -ing)

**A. Spot the error**
1. I usually am going to the café after school. → *I usually **go** to the café after school.*
2. She go there every weekend. → *She **goes** there every weekend.*
3. We prefer to meeting at the store. → *We prefer **to meet / meeting** at the store.*
4. Do you like to studying here? → *Do you like **to study / studying** here?*
5. They are never late usually. → *They are **never** late.* (drop the clashing *usually*)
6. He don't like crowded places. → *He **doesn't** like crowded places.*

**B. Transform**
1. (always / I / study / in the library) → *I always study in the library.*
2. (she / never / drink / coffee) → *She never drinks coffee.*
3. "I like tea. I don't like coffee." (use *prefer*) → *I prefer tea to coffee.*
4. (how often / you / come here?) → *How often do you come here?*
5. "He likes to play games." (use *love*) → *He loves playing games. / loves to play games.*
6. (we / usually / meet / at the konbini) → *We usually meet at the konbini.*

**C. Gap-fill** — present simple / frequency
"My friends and I __(1: meet)__ at the same café most days. I __(2)__ arrive first (frequency: 90%), because I __(3: prefer)__ the window seat. We __(4)__ stay after nine (frequency: 0%). Lena __(5: not like)__ noisy places, so she __(6: choose)__ the quiet corner."
**Key:** 1 meet · 2 usually · 3 prefer · 4 never · 5 doesn't like · 6 chooses

**D. Build your own** (frames): *I usually ___ . / I never ___ . / I prefer ___ to ___ . / How often do you ___ ?*

## UNIT 2 · L5 — Describing & Comparing (comparatives · superlatives · *as…as*)

**A. Spot the error**
1. The desert is more drier than the forest. → *The desert is **drier** than the forest.* (double comparative)
2. A whale is bigger as a fish. → *A whale is bigger **than** a fish.*
3. This is the most tall tree in the park. → *This is the **tallest** tree in the park.*
4. Migration is more hard than it looks. → *Migration is **harder** than it looks.*
5. The river is as clean than before. → *The river is as clean **as** before.*
6. Cities are the more crowded place. → *Cities are the **most crowded places**.*

**B. Transform** (comparative / superlative / *as…as*)
1. (desert / dry / forest) → *The desert is drier than the forest.*
2. (this cactus / tall / that one) → *This cactus is taller than that one.*
3. (the blue whale / large / animal / world) → *The blue whale is the largest animal in the world.*
4. (a city / not quiet / a village) → *A city is not as quiet as a village.*
5. (rivers / important / roads / for wildlife) → *Rivers are more important than roads for wildlife.*
6. (which / dangerous / journey?) → *Which is the most dangerous journey?*

**C. Gap-fill** — complete the comparison
"A rainforest is much __(1: wet)__ than a desert, but a desert can be __(2: hot)__ during the day. The cheetah is the __(3: fast)__ land animal, faster __(4)__ any other. However, it is not __(5)__ strong __(6)__ a lion."
**Key:** 1 wetter · 2 hotter · 3 fastest · 4 than · 5 as · 6 as

**D. Build your own** (frames): *___ is bigger/smaller than ___ . / The ___est ___ is ___ . / A ___ is not as ___ as a ___ .*

## UNIT 2 · L8 — Reporting Objectively (the passive · formal/objective register)

**A. Spot the error**
1. The river was cleaned by last year. → *The river was cleaned last year.* (stray *by*)
2. Trees are plant in the city. → *Trees are **planted** in the city.*
3. The report were written by scientists. → *The report **was** written by scientists.*
4. Many animals is protected now. → *Many animals **are** protected now.*
5. I think the forest should be save. → *The forest should be **saved**.* (objective register: drop *I think*; use participle)
6. The water is polluting by factories. → *The water is **polluted** by factories.*

**B. Transform** (active → passive)
1. Volunteers planted the trees. → *The trees were planted (by volunteers).*
2. People pollute the river. → *The river is polluted (by people).*
3. The council will protect the wetland. → *The wetland will be protected (by the council).*
4. Scientists have studied the birds. → *The birds have been studied (by scientists).*
5. Someone must clean the beach. → *The beach must be cleaned.*
6. They release the fish into the river. → *The fish are released into the river.*

**C. Gap-fill** — put the verb in the passive
"Every year, thousands of trees __(1: cut down)__ for building. However, in some cities new forests __(2: create)__ on rooftops. The old river __(3: clean)__ in 2019, and fish __(4: reintroduce)__ soon after. Today the area __(5: protect)__ by law."
**Key:** 1 are cut down · 2 are created · 3 was cleaned · 4 were reintroduced · 5 is protected

**D. Build your own** (report frames, no *I think*): *The area is ___ . / Steps have been ___ to ___ . / The species must be ___ . / It is ___ that ___ .*
