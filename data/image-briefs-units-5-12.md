# Image briefs — Units 5–12

Pre-brief for image sourcing. Rules (same as Units 1–4): **CC0 / PD / CC-BY only · no identifiable faces** (distant / from behind / silhouette / hands-only OK) · no brand logo as subject · warm documentary look · portrait for hero/wayin panels · every image serves the lesson's teaching point. **L1 (orientation) and L2 (vocabulary) have no image slots.** Reading lessons get hero + wayin; grammar / listening / writing / speaking lessons get a hero. Verify each image by eye when sourcing.
