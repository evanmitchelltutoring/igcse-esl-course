# Reading glossaries + vocabulary-in-context tasks (Units 1–2)

**P1.3 + P3.9.** For every reading passage: a **"tricky words" strip** (put on the reading slide — 4 words the say-it-first vocab doesn't pre-teach, each a one-line gloss, so EAL students aren't blocked mid-text) and a **vocabulary-in-context micro-task** (a real Cambridge sub-skill: *find the word/phrase in the text that means…* — 3 items, keyed). Keep the strip small and above/beside the text; run the micro-task after the first read.

---
## UNIT 1

### L2 · "The Café That Never Asks You to Leave"
**Tricky words:** *hovering* = staying annoyingly close, waiting · *an unwritten rule* = a rule everyone follows but no one states · *spectacular* = very impressive, dramatic · *concentration* = the ability to focus.
**Find the word that means…** 1) "impressive and exciting" → *spectacular* · 2) "waiting too close, ready to interrupt" → *hovering* · 3) "the ability to keep your attention on something" → *concentration*.

### L3 · "My Corner"
**Tricky words:** *crooked* = not straight · *chipped* = with a small piece broken off · *uneven* = not level or smooth · *garlands* = rings of flowers.
**Find the word that means…** 1) "not straight" → *crooked* · 2) "not flat or level" → *uneven* · 3) "a cup with a small broken edge" → *chipped* (cup).

### L5 · "The Shop That Became a Living Room"
**Tricky words:** *unofficial* = not formal or arranged · *loitering* = standing around with no clear purpose · *a nuisance* = something annoying · *permission* = being allowed to do something.
**Find the word that means…** 1) "standing around with no purpose" → *loitering* · 2) "not planned or official" → *unofficial* · 3) "being allowed to be somewhere" → *permission*.

### L8 · "The Indoor City"
**Tricky words:** *the default* = the usual, automatic choice · *generous* = giving a lot, freely · *a backdrop* = the background · *window-shopping* = looking at shops without buying.
**Find the word that means…** 1) "the normal, automatic choice" → *the default* · 2) "looking at goods without buying" → *window-shopping* · 3) "giving freely, expecting nothing back" → *generous*.

### L10 · "The Corner That Closed"
**Tricky words:** *premium* = high-quality and expensive · *in-between places* = neither home nor a shop — the spaces between · *to discourage* = to make someone not want to do something · *papered over* = covered with paper (a closed shopfront).
**Find the word that means…** 1) "expensive and high-quality" → *premium* · 2) "to put someone off doing something" → *discourage* · 3) "covered over with paper" → *papered over*.

## UNIT 2

### L3 · "The Plants That Break the Rules"
**Tricky words:** *a living fossil* = a species that has barely changed for millions of years · *to unfurl* = to open out / unroll · *resurrection* = coming back to life · *specimens* = individual examples of a plant or animal.
**Find the word that means…** 1) "to open out or unroll" → *unfurl(s)* · 2) "coming back to life" → *resurrection* · 3) "a species almost unchanged for millions of years" → *a living fossil*.

### L4 · "Four Great Journeys"
**Tricky words:** *to breed* = to produce young · *generations* = stages in a family line (parents, children, grandchildren) · *complex* = complicated, with many parts · *to snatch* = to grab quickly.
**Find the word that means…** 1) "to produce babies/young" → *breed(s)* · 2) "to grab something quickly" → *snatch(ing)* · 3) "complicated, with many parts" → *complex*.

### L6 · "The River That Came Back to Life"
**Tricky words:** *a drain* = a channel that carries away waste water · *a floodplain* = the flat land a river spreads onto in heavy rain · *neglected* = not cared for · *polluters* = those who make water/air dirty.
**Find the word that means…** 1) "flat land a river floods onto" → *floodplain* · 2) "not looked after" → *neglected* · 3) "a channel for dirty water" → *a drain*.

### L7 · "The Man Who Grows Forests on Rooftops"
**Tricky words:** *sceptical* = doubtful, not easily convinced · *cultivation* = the growing of crops · *overloaded* = too full to cope · *charity* = help given free, out of kindness.
**Find the word that means…** 1) "doubtful, hard to convince" → *sceptical* · 2) "too full to work properly" → *overloaded* (drains) · 3) "the growing of crops" → *cultivation*.

---
**Rollout:** build the same two elements for each later unit's readings — 4 un-pretaught words + a 3-item "find the word" task, answers teacher-only.
