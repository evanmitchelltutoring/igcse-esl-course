# Reading glossaries + vocabulary-in-context tasks (Units 3–12)

**P1.3 + P3.9.** For every reading passage: a **"tricky words" strip** (put on the reading slide — 4 words the say-it-first vocab doesn't pre-teach, each a one-line gloss, so EAL students aren't blocked mid-text) and a **vocabulary-in-context micro-task** (a real Cambridge sub-skill: *find the word/phrase in the text that means…* — 3 items, keyed). Keep the strip small and above/beside the text; run the micro-task after the first read. Every answer is a word or phrase that literally appears in that passage; answers are teacher-only.
