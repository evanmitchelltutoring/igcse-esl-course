# Unit 4 image briefs — "Cultures & Connections / Around the World"

Pre-brief for the 15 image slots (opener + reading lessons hero+wayin + grammar/listening/writing heroes). Theme: travel, belonging, language and identity across cultures. Same rules as Units 1–3: CC0/PD/CC-BY only, **no identifiable faces** (distant / from behind / silhouette / hands only OK), no brand logo as subject, warm documentary look, portrait for hero/wayin panels, every image serves the lesson's teaching point. When Unit 4's decks exist, source straight from this list (verify each by eye).

| Slot | Orient. | Subject & teaching purpose |
|---|---|---|
| u4-opener-hero | portrait | Unit cover — cultures & connections: a vibrant global scene (a busy world market, a diverse street, a traveller's wide view of an unfamiliar city). Alive, warm, no faces. |
| u4-opener-apply | landscape | "A place that shaped you": a person **from behind/distant** looking out over a new place — a traveller at a viewpoint, arriving somewhere. |
| u4-l3-hero | portrait | "The Tourist Gaze" (48 hours in a city): a tourist's-eye view — a visitor (from behind) before a landmark, or a camera raised at a city. The *surface* view of a place. |
| u4-l3-wayin | portrait | A tourist detail: a folded map, a camera, a guidebook, a passport with stamps — the tools of the short visit. |
| u4-l4-hero | portrait | "Living Elsewhere" (reading for feeling — the month I stopped translating): the quiet feeling of *living* somewhere foreign — an everyday local street at dusk, a neighbourhood market, ordinary life abroad. |
| u4-l4-wayin | portrait | A sensory "settling-in" detail: a foreign street sign, a local café table, a market stall you'd pass daily. |
| u4-l5-hero | portrait | "Code-Switching / Two Languages, One Self": language & identity — a bilingual sign, two scripts side by side, or a figure between two worlds (from behind). |
| u4-l5-wayin | portrait | A bilingual detail: a dual-language street sign, two dictionaries, subtitles on a screen. |
| u4-l6-hero | portrait | "What Stays With You / Coming Home Changed" (reverse culture shock): coming home yet feeling in-between — an airport walkway, a suitcase by a familiar door, a departures board. |
| u4-l6-wayin | portrait | A "coming home" detail: a packed bag, a boarding pass, a doorway/threshold. |
| u4-l7-hero | portrait | Grammar — Present Perfect (life experiences, "I have visited…"): a wall of travel photos, passport stamps, a world map stuck with pins. |
| u4-l8-hero | portrait | Grammar — Defining Relative Clauses ("a place where…", "a person who…"): a describable cultural subject — a market stall, a craftsperson's hands at work, a distinctive building. |
| u4-l9-hero | portrait | Listening — "Voices of Travel and Return": interviews/voices — headphones over a world map, a recorder, a travel-podcast feel. |
| u4-l10-hero | portrait | Writing — "Directed Writing: The Article": an article/magazine spread, a keyboard beside travel notes and a coffee — writing for a reader. |
| u4-l11-hero | portrait | Writing — "A Place That Shaped You" (reflective): a single evocative, personal place — a quiet landscape or street that feels like it holds a memory. |

**Note:** L1 (orientation) and L2 (vocabulary launch) have no image slots, mirroring Units 1–3.
