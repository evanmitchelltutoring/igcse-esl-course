One word per gap — all four families

1. Move: Stacking a habit onto a trigger makes the movement ______.
2. Fuel: Even mild ______ can feel like hunger.
3. Mind: The ability to recover quickly from difficulty is ______.
4. Rest: A ______ bedtime helps more than one long lie-in.
5. Fuel: Sweets are fine in ______ — the problem is too many.
6. Mind: The advert's promise was ______ — it gave a wrong impression.
Stuck? → the family label tells you which set of words to search your memory for.
Push further → write one more gap-fill of your own and swap with a partner.
Answer · mark your own

One word per gap

1. …makes the movement automatic.
2. Even mild dehydration can feel like hunger.
3. …is resilience.
4. A consistent (or steady) bedtime helps more.
5. Sweets are fine in moderation.
6. The advert's promise was misleading.
