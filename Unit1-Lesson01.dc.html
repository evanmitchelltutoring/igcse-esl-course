The idea · copy this into your notes

What makes a place a third place?

A third place is a public place — not home, not school — that you choose, and where you feel you belong.
A third place usually…
  • welcomes you without asking much
  • is cheap or free to be in
  • doesn't pressure you to leave
  • has regulars — familiar faces
…and it makes you feel
  • relaxed — you can unwind
  • safe — like a refuge
  • part of something — you belong

Six words for places & belonging

/bɪˈlɒŋ·ɪŋ/ · noun

/ˈæm·bi·əns/ · noun

/ˈlɪŋ·ɡə/ · verb

unwind
/ʌnˈwaɪnd/ · verb

cosy
/ˈkəʊ·zi/ · adjective

refuge
/ˈref·juːdʒ/ · noun

How to describe your third place

My third place is the corner table of a small café near my home. I go there to read and mark work. It makes me feel calm, because no one asks anything of me and the ambience is quiet.

1Your bedroom, where you relax after school
2Your classroom, where you have lessons

Answer

Number 3 — the library steps

It's chosen, public, and social — that's a third place.
1 · Bedroom — that's home, your first place.
2 · Classroom & 4 · Weekend job — places you must be: second places.
