The idea behind the unit

Why do living things
need us?

Every plant and animal lives in a habitat — and when habitats are damaged, whole ecosystems can collapse.

From migrating birds to city trees, living things depend on their environment. This unit explores how nature survives, how humans threaten it, and how conservation can bring it back.

How we annotate every text

Three colours, three questions

Every time we read closely, we mark in these three colours.

Teal
Meaning
What does the text say? The facts, the point, the plain sense.
Terracotta

How does the writer say it? The word chosen, the image, the effect.
Plum
Response
How does it make the reader feel or react? Your honest reaction.

Where the unit is heading

Capstone: Protect a Living Thing

In your group, choose a plant, animal or wild place that matters. Describe it, explain the threat, and make the case for protecting it — then present your campaign to the class.

Describe
What is it, where does it live, and what habitat does it need?
Explain
What threatens it — pollution, building, drought — and why it matters?
Persuade
Make your call to action: what can ordinary people do to help?
