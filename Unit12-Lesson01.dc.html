The idea · copy this into your notes

The battle is between panic and composure

Nearly every survival story turns on one thing: whether the person keeps a clear head. Panic narrows your thinking and burns energy; composure keeps your options open long enough to make a good decision.
Panic
Rushes, wanders, gives up. "I ran without thinking and got more lost."
Composure
Stops, thinks, chooses one plan. "I made myself stop and follow the water downhill."

A challenge I've faced

Once, on a hike, the mist came down and my group lost the path. What frightened me most was how fast the light was going. I forced myself to stop instead of panicking, and we waited together until it lifted — looking back, I realise stopping was the bravest thing we did.
