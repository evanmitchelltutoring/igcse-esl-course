The rule · copy this into your notes

Retell it: one step back

say vs tell
tell + person: She told me…
say (no person): She said (that)…
backshift · one tense back
is → was · like → liked · will → would · bought → had bought
Also change: pronouns (I → she, my → her) and time words (tomorrow → the next day, yesterday → the day before).
Questions: use statement order, no do/did — "Do you like it?" → She asked if I liked it.  ·  Commands: tell + person + to — "Try it." → She told me to try it.

Practice 1 · spot the error

Find and fix one mistake in each

1. She said me that she liked my scarf.
2. He told that he was tired.
3. She said she will buy the dress.
4. He said he is happy with his new look.
5. She asked me what did I want.
6. He said me to try the jacket.
Support → check three things: say/tell, backshift, and question word order.
Stretch → which two are about say/tell, and which is a reported question?
Answer · mark your own

Find and fix

1. She told me / She said that she liked my scarf. (say takes no object)
2. He said that he was tired. / He told me that… (tell needs an object)
3. She said she would buy the dress. (will → would)
4. He said he was happy with his new look. (is → was)
5. She asked me what I wanted. (statement order, no did)
6. He told me to try the jacket. (command = tell + object + to)

Practice 2 · transform

Rewrite each as reported speech

1. "I like your shoes," she said.
2. "We are going to the show," they said.
3. "I will wear black tomorrow," he said.
4. "I bought this yesterday," she told me.
5. "Do you like my hat?" he asked.
6. "Try the smaller size," the assistant told me.
Support → change three things every time: the verb (back one), the pronoun, and any time word.
Stretch → Q5 is a question and Q6 a command — remember their special patterns.
Answer · mark your own

Rewrite as reported speech

1. She said (that) she liked my shoes.
2. They said (that) they were going to the show.
3. He said (that) he would wear black the next day.
4. She told me (that) she had bought that the day before.
5. He asked (me) whether/if I liked his hat.
6. The assistant told me to try the smaller size.

Practice 3 · gap-fill in context

Complete the reported speech

Yesterday my friend Leo showed me his new jacket. He said (that) he __(1)__ bought it at a vintage market. He told __(2)__ that the jacket __(3)__ over thirty years old. I asked him __(4)__ he had paid a lot, and he said it __(5)__ cheap. He also said he __(6)__ wear it to the party the next day.
Answer · mark your own

Gap-fill

He said he had (1) bought it at a vintage market. He told me (2) that the jacket was (3) over thirty years old. I asked him whether/if (4) he had paid a lot, and he said it was (5) cheap. He also said he would (6) wear it to the party the next day.
