The skill · how to learn a word

Never learn a word alone

A word you learn on its own is easy to forget. Learn it with its family and one partner word it likes to sit beside — and it sticks.
Word family
One idea, many forms:
innovate → innovation → innovative.
Knowing the family means you can use the idea as a verb, a noun or an adjective.
Collocation (partner word)
Words that go together sound natural:
allocate funding · a costly setback · a scientific breakthrough.

Eight words, each with a partner

allocate (verb)
give money for a purpose · allocate funding
opportunity cost (noun)
what you give up to choose this · a high opportunity cost
spin-off (noun)
a useful side-benefit · an unexpected spin-off
breakthrough (noun)
a major discovery · a scientific breakthrough
squander (verb)
waste carelessly · squander resources
prioritise (verb)
treat as most important · prioritise needs at home
feasible (adj)
possible to do · technically feasible
pioneering (adj)
first to try something new · pioneering research
