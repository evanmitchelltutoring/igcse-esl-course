Way In · sound like a friend

Which one is a letter to a friend?

REPORT — "The wetland is of significant ecological value and requires protection."
LETTER — "You won't believe the wetland I saw — it's the most alive place I've ever been, and it's in danger!"

A friendly letter is warm, personal, and full of feeling — that's your job today.

A Grade-A letter · nature
Where & what What you saw Why it matters
Dear Maya, I have to tell you about the wetland I visited last weekend — it's the most alive place I've ever seen. At dawn a whole flock of birds lifted off the water together, and the reeds were so lush you could hardly see the far bank. But our guide said the wetland is fragile: new building nearby threatens the birds' habitat, and without protection the whole ecosystem could decline. I think we should join the conservation group's clean-up next month — this is somewhere worth saving. Write back soon and tell me you're in! Love, Sam

Register · sound informal

The marks of a friendly letter

Use
Dear ___, · contractions (it's, you'll)
"You won't believe…"
a question or exclamation
"Write back soon!" · Love, ___
Avoid
"Dear Sir/Madam"
"is of significant value"
"I am writing to inform you"
a cold, distant tone
