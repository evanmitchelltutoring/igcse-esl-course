A model essay · watch the P-R-R-E

"Everyone should keep learning for life." — how far do you agree?

In a world that never stops changing, I firmly believe that everyone should keep learning for life.

Firstly, lifelong learning helps us stay relevant at work. Because industries have been changing rapidly, old skills can quickly become obsolete. As a result, people who keep learning are far more likely to keep their jobs. My uncle, for example, decided to retrain in computing at forty and avoided falling behind.

Secondly, continuing to learn helps us adapt and reach our potential. Learning new things builds a growth mindset. This means we face challenges with confidence. I have seen this in my grandmother, who chose to adapt by learning video calls and now speaks to relatives abroad every day.

In conclusion, learning should never stop. Those who have been learning throughout their lives will always be ready for whatever the future brings.

Position — a clear view
P-R-R-E #1 (relevant at work)
P-R-R-E #2 (reach our potential)
Conclusion — looks forward
