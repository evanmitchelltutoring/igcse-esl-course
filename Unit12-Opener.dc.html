The big idea

An adventure story is really about decisions under pressure.

This unit uses true-feeling survival stories to teach real skills: following an account under pressure, weighing risk, recounting an ordeal, and reporting on safety. The heroes rarely do anything dramatic — they keep calm, keep moving, and endure.
