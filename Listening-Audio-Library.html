Unit 11 · Mystery & the Unexplained — Lesson 8

Track 1 (The Vanishing Village) and Track 2 (The Ghost Ship).

Show script
1930, northern Canada (Angikuni). Reportedly found: smouldering fires, half-eaten food, starved tethered sled dogs; ~30 people gone. Later investigators found no official records or reliable news reports. Most researchers: exaggerated or invented — a myth, not stated fact.
Show script
Mary Celeste, December 1872, found adrift in good condition; crew and captain's family gone. Missing lifeboat = left deliberately. Favoured explanation: feared an alcohol-fume explosion, abandoned ship, lost at sea. Never confirmed.

Unit 12 · Adventure & Survival — Lesson 8

Track 1 (lost in the woods) and Track 2 (a mountain rescue interview).

Show script
Rules with reasons: STOP (walking gets you more lost/injured); shelter (body heat); water over food (weeks vs days); stay calm (panic wastes energy). Most lost people found in 24–48h if they stay put.
Show script
Most rescued: the unprepared, not the reckless. Biggest factor: the weather. Best single action: check the forecast and believe it. If lost: stop, stay put, keep warm, call for help. Rescuers are modest volunteers.
