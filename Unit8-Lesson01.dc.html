The idea · copy this into your notes

Three things to weigh

There is no simple answer. To decide whether space is worth it, you have to weigh three things against each other — all unit we'll gather evidence on each.
Why we reach
wonder, curiosity, ambition, inspiration
What it costs
money, risk, and the chance to spend it here
What we get back
satellites, spin-offs, real breakthroughs

Answer · mark your own

Which phrase fits?

1. Admittedly, the same money could build hundreds of hospitals.
2. Space is costly; on the other hand, it has given us satellites we rely on daily.
3. For most scientists, the benefits outweigh the costs.
4. All things considered, I believe the wonder is worth the price.
Watch me first · a model answer

My first view on the question

Admittedly, space is enormously costly, and on the one hand that money could help people here now. All things considered, though, I think the benefits outweigh the costs — the technology it returns helps everyone.
