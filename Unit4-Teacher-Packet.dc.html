4 · Reading tasks

4a · "Forty-Eight Hours in a City I Never Met" — short answers
Answer key · 4a1 sixty (pictures).   2 the river cruise was cancelled (rain).   3 the small café — the owner who drew a map on a napkin.   4 because nobody photographs a stranger drawing a map.   5 the real character of a place lies below the surface, in parts no itinerary makes time for. Mark for precision; over-long answers with wrong extra detail can cancel.
4b · "The Month I Stopped Translating" — reading for feeling
Answer key · 4b1 they translated every sentence in their head first, so the moment passed.   2 guilt / failure — "accusation" makes the ordinary evening feel like a charge that they had wasted the day.   3 they laughed at a joke without translating it.   4 belonging came gradually and unnoticed — "no edges", not one clear moment. Full credit needs the feeling named AND the word/effect.
4c · "Two Languages, One Self" — interview
Answer key · 4c1 louder / quicker to joke in one, gentler in the other — both are her.   2 "making a mess."   3 that she is half of two things instead of fully something new.   4 being between two cultures is a whole identity of its own, not a failure to belong to one side.
4d · "Coming Home Changed" — note completion
Answer key · 4dStages: honeymoon → frustration → (gradual) readjustment.   Ease it: friends made abroad · a returnee / online group · a habit (food, phrase, routine).   How long: the deeper the immersion / longer away, the longer the readjustment. Accept short paraphrase; 1–2 words.

6 · Writing

6a · Article "Lessons from Away" (Experience → Reflection → Resonance)
Stems: "I have lived / stayed in…" · "What surprised me was…" · "It taught me that…" · "Even now, when…" Present perfect + 5 target words.
6b · Description "A Place That Shaped You" (Scene → Detail → Verdict)
Show, don't tell: sensory detail + a defining relative clause; end on what the place made of you.

9b · "Watch for" — misconceptions specific to this unit
Present perfect (L7)Base verb for participle ("have visit"); since + a length ("since two years"); adverb misplaced. Drill for + length vs since + start, and ever/never/just/already between have and the participle.
Relative clauses (L8)Keeping the repeated word ("which it", "where… there") and who/which/where mismatch. Drill: people → who, things → which, places → where; drop the repeat.
Reading for feeling (L4)Stopping at "it's sad". Require name-it-then-prove-it: the feeling AND the word/image that carries it.
Short answers (L3)Over-writing. In Part 1, extra wrong detail can cancel a correct answer — answer, then delete what wasn't asked.
Description (L11)Telling with adjectives ("it was nice/old") instead of showing with sensory detail. Insist on things the reader can see, hear or smell.
Register (L10–11)Article/description drifting into a bare list of events. Require reflection (Resonance) and a verdict — what the place taught or made of the writer.
9c · Support / Stretch
Support — give the frames: "I have ___ since ___." · "…is a place where ___." · "What surprised me was ___." Word bank: settle in · unfamiliar · gradually · nostalgia · belonging · perspective · adapt. Provide the article/description skeleton pre-labelled so students only fill content.
Stretch — combine present perfect and a relative clause in one sentence ("This is the street where I have lived for ten years"); replace every "told" adjective with a "shown" detail; deliver a 60-second spoken version of the description.
