Exam skill · the listening routine

Read → predict → meaning → check

Read
the question & options before the clip.
Predict
what a speaker might say for each.
Meaning
listen for the idea, not the exact word.
Check
use the second play to confirm.
The trap: a wrong option often repeats a word you just heard. The right one usually says the same idea in different words.

Exercise 3 · Part 3 · complete the notes

No more than three words per gap

How an AI learns to make pictures:
• It is not born able to draw — it learns by __(1)__.
• The builders gather a huge __(2)__ of images.
• They often __(3)__ them automatically from the internet.
• It does not memorise images; it works out __(4)__.
• You give it a __(5)__, and it generates a new image.
Stretch → in one sentence, why does the speaker say "the fairness questions are far from settled"?
Exercise 3 · answers

Completed notes

• …it learns by example (1).
• The builders gather a huge dataset (2) of images.
• They often scrape (3) them automatically from the internet.
• It works out patterns (4).
• You give it a prompt (5), and it generates a new image.

Exercise 5 · Part 5 · interview · choose A, B or C

Painter vs developer

1. The painter says art is mainly about…   A beauty   B intention   C speed   → __
2. The developer says the program learns…   A by memorising images   B by studying examples, as the painter did   C with no human input   → __
3. Both agree the fair criticism is that training artists were…   A too slow   B not asked or paid   C not skilled   → __
4. To help a buyer or judge, the developer suggests…   A banning AI   B honest labelling   C higher prices   → __
5. The middle ground they both accept is…   A a separate category for AI art   B no prizes at all   C only human judges   → __
Support → listen for where they agree — Q3 and Q5 are agreement moments.
Stretch → whose argument did you find stronger, and why?
Exercise 5 · answers

Painter vs developer

1 → B intention — "a person choosing to say something".
2 → B by studying examples, as the painter did.
3 → B not asked or paid.
4 → B honest labelling (transparency).
5 → A a separate category for AI art.
