Engine 1 · the concession paragraph

G-C-S: Grant → Counter → Stand

Grant
Admit the other side's best point. "There is no denying that AI produces work that moves people."
Counter
Turn it. "And yet beauty alone is not art."
Stand
Return to your view, now stronger for being fair.
notebook
Engine 2 · the reasoning paragraph

E-S-V: Evidence → Speculation → Verdict

Evidence
A fact from the reading. "A program only predicts patterns it has learned."
Speculation (graded)
Reason with a modal. "It might copy the look of art, but it cannot mean it."
Verdict
Land the claim. "Without intention, this calls into question whether it is art at all."

Argument bank · use at least five

Chunks that raise the register

Argument phrases
• it could be argued that
• this calls into question
• there is no denying that
• the heart of the matter is
Key terms
• intention — a person choosing to say something
• authentic — genuine, real
• legitimate — proper, deserving to count
• merit — real quality or worth
Weave, don't bolt: a chunk must fit the sentence's meaning — not sit on top of it.
