The task · read it twice
Your friend feels tired and stressed all the time and keeps saying they "have no time" to look after themselves. The free MoveWell Weekend is next month. Write a letter persuading them to come with you — and to make one small, healthy change now.

Register · write like a friend, not a form

The tone is everything here

✗ Too formal
"Dear Sam, I am writing to persuade you to attend the MoveWell Weekend. It offers numerous benefits…"
Reads like a letter to a stranger. No warmth, no you.
✓ Warm & personal
"Hi Sam, I've been meaning to write since you said you'd been feeling worn out. Honestly, I've been a bit worried…"
Greeting, contractions, care. This is the register that scores.

Warm-up · advice + a reason

Finish each with a result — "so…" or "that way…"

1. Why don't you come with me? It's really relaxed, so ______.
2. It's worth swapping one late night for an early one — that way ______.
3. You don't need to be sporty at all, which means ______.
Stuck? → ask "what good thing happens if they do it?" — that's your result.
Push further → slip a target word in — steady, recovery, balanced.
Answer · example results

Yours may differ — these all work

1. …so there's no pressure and we can just go at our own steady pace.
2. …that way your body gets the recovery it needs instead of relying on caffeine.
3. …which means you won't feel discouraged before you even start.

Plan · three short paragraphs

One content point per paragraph

¶1 · Open + your worry
Greet warmly. Say kindly what you've noticed — because you care.
¶2 · Make it un-scary
Describe the weekend as relaxed and beginner-friendly. Answer "I'm not sporty."
¶3 · Persuade + one step
Invite them to come with you. Offer one tiny change to start today.
Use at least 4 target words:   recovery · steady · balanced · discouraged · a balanced approach · rely on

A model letter

Read it · then write your own

Hi Sam,

I've been meaning to write since you told me how worn out you've been feeling. Honestly, I've been a little worried about you — you keep saying you've no time for yourself, and lately you've sounded really discouraged.

Have you thought about coming to the MoveWell Weekend with me next month? Before you say no — it's nothing intense, and you don't need to be sporty at all. It's relaxed and beginner-friendly: gentle walks, stretching, and tips on sleep and recovery. People just go at their own steady pace.

Why don't we try it together? It's worth giving it a go, because a balanced approach really does help. And even before then, it's worth swapping one late night for an early one — that way your body rests instead of having to rely on caffeine.

Just think about it for me? Write back soon. Take care, Alex

¶1 — worry, warmth (point 1)
¶2 — un-scary picture (point 2)
¶3 — invite + one step (point 3)
