Structure 1 · if… then…

Three conditionals, three jobs

First
a real future possibility
If it rains, we will turn back.
Second
unreal or unlikely now
If I were you, I would wait.
Third
regret about the past
If we had checked, we wouldn't have been trapped.
notebook
Structure 2 · what they said

Reported speech: shift back one step

When you report what someone said, move the tense back one step and change the pronouns to match.
Direct → reported
present → past · is → was
past → past perfect · went → had gone
will → would · can → could
In action
"I am turning back," she said.
→ She said she was turning back.

"We will wait," he said.
→ He said they would wait.
Compare · weak vs strong

Same events — sharper grammar

Weak
"We didn't check the weather. We got trapped. My uncle told me to always check it."
Flat past simple; no regret marked; advice quoted flatly.
Strong
"If we had checked the forecast, we would never have been trapped. My uncle had always told me to check it, and he was right."
Third conditional carries the regret; reported speech retells the advice.

Try it · conditional or reported?

Complete each sentence

1. If you (get) ______ lost, follow water downhill. (real advice — 1st)
2. If I (be) ______ you, I would turn back now. (2nd)
3. If she had rested, she ______ up again. (3rd — regret)
4. "I am cold," he said. → He said he ______ cold. (reported)
5. "We will wait for morning," they said. → They said they ______ for morning. (reported)
Support → the label in brackets tells you which structure to use.

Conditional or reported?

1. If you get lost, follow water downhill. (1st)
2. If I were you, I would turn back. (2nd)
3. …she might never have got up again. (3rd)
4. He said he was cold. (present → past)
5. They said they would wait for morning. (will → would)
