notebook
The rule · copy this into your notes

What does each one attach to?

although / even though
+ a full clause (subject + verb)
"Although it is fake, it looks real."
despite / in spite of
+ a noun or -ing
"Despite being fake, it looks real."
however
starts a new sentence (; or .)
"It's fake; however, it looks real."
Three traps:  ✗ Although… but… (pick one)  ·  ✗ despite of (no of)  ·  ✗ however used to join a clause (it is not a subordinator).

Practice 1 · spot the error

Find and fix one mistake in each

1. Although the picture is fake, but it looks real.
2. Despite it was made by AI, people liked it.
3. However the app is free, many artists dislike it.
4. She is talented, despite she is young.
5. Despite of the criticism, he kept painting.
6. Although it is expensive, however the software is popular.
Support → each breaks one of the three traps — spot which trap first.
Stretch → for 2 and 4, give both a despite fix and an although fix.
Answer · mark your own

Find and fix

1. Although the picture is fake, it looks real. (no although … but)
2. Despite being made by AI, people liked it. (despite + -ing, not a clause)
3. Although the app is free, many artists dislike it. (however can't join a clause)
4. She is talented although / even though she is young. (despite can't take a clause)
5. Despite / In spite of the criticism, he kept painting. (no of after despite)
6. Although it is expensive, the software is popular. (don't pair although with however)
