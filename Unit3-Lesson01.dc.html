Wellbeing is a balance

Wellbeing means feeling healthy in body and mind — kept in balance across the things you do every day.

Answer · mark your own

Which phrase fits?

1. Skipping sleep can lead to poor concentration the next day.
2. There is growing evidence that exercise improves mood, not just fitness.
3. A crash diet is the very opposite of a healthy plan.
4. In the long run, small daily habits beat one big effort.
Watch me first · a model answer

What "being healthy" means to me

To me, being healthy is really about balance. There is growing evidence that sleep matters as much as food, so in the long run I try to rest well, because a tired mind can lead to bad choices.
