Exam skill · the listening routine

Read → predict → meaning → check

Read
the question & options before the clip.
Predict
what a speaker might say for each.
Meaning
listen for the idea, not the exact word.
Check
use the second play to confirm.
The trap: a wrong option often repeats a word you just heard. The right one usually says the same idea in different words.

Exercise 3 · Part 3 · complete the notes

One or two words per gap

The thirty-item challenge:
• For one whole year, she wore only __(1)__ pieces of clothing.
• She started because her wardrobe was __(2)__ and she kept buying things worn once.
• The rule for anything new: __(3)__, one out.
• The biggest surprise was that __(4)__.
• Now, before buying, she asks if it will __(5)__.
Stretch → in one sentence, what does she mean by "the audience was mostly in my own head"?
Exercise 3 · answers

Completed notes

• …she wore only thirty (1) pieces of clothing.
• Her wardrobe was too full (2) and she kept buying things worn once.
• The rule: one in (3), one out.
• The biggest surprise: no one noticed (4).
• Now she asks if a new item will earn its place (5).

Exercise 5 · Part 5 · interview · choose A, B or C

Niran and the clothes-swap

1. The clothes-swap began…   A as a school project   B by accident, from trading with a friend   C from a teacher's idea   → __
2. Niran's first reason for starting was to…   A save the planet   B save money   C be fashionable   → __
3. On whether swaps are a passing trend, he thinks…   A the habit will last even if the word fades   B they'll disappear soon   C they're already over   → __
4. Does he miss buying new things?   A never   B sometimes, but the thrill fades fast   C all the time   → __
5. To someone who thinks their choices are too small, he says…   A small choices add up when many join in   B one person can't help   C stop trying   → __
Support → listen for the honest admissions — he concedes before he argues.
Stretch → where does Niran use a concession (Lesson 8) — admit something, then turn?
Exercise 5 · answers

Niran and the clothes-swap

1 → B by accident, from trading with a friend.
2 → B to save money (the environmental part came later).
3 → A the habit will last even if the word fades.
4 → B sometimes, but the thrill fades fast.
5 → A small choices add up when many join in.

Notice Q2 and Q4: Niran concedes first ("I won't pretend…", "sometimes I miss it") — the concession move from Lesson 8.
