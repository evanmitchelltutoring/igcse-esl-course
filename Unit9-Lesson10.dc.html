Decode the task · before you write

The task, in plain words

You've found a great study spot — a quiet library corner, a café, a community space — and it has helped you. Write an email (120–160 words) telling a friend, in a friendly, informal tone.
The spot
What is it, and why do you like it?
How it helped
Be specific — what's changed in your studying?
The invite
Ask them to come — and say when.

The shape · four beats

Greeting → the spot → how it helped → invite

Greeting
"Hi Mia!" — a warm hook, not "Dear Mia".
The spot
What/where it is, why you love it. Gerund: "I love working there."
How it helped
Perfect continuous: "I've been studying there every day, and my focus has improved."
Invite + sign-off
"Come with me on Saturday?" then a warm close: "Love, …".

Weak → strong · warm it up

Too stiff, then friendly

Weak (too formal)
"Dear friend, I am writing to inform you of a study location. It is satisfactory. I would recommend attending."
Reads like a business letter — wrong register.
Strong (warm)
"Hey Mia! Guess what — I've found the perfect place to study, and I had to tell you. It's this quiet library corner and I love working there."
Greeting, contractions, real enthusiasm.

Words to use · at least three

Words that make it warm and specific

accessible
easy to get to — "it's free and totally accessible"
motivation
the drive to study — "it gives me so much motivation"
self-directed
working on your own plan — "it's easy to be self-directed"
supportive · milestone
"the staff are supportive" · "I hit a milestone yesterday"
A model email · watch the four beats

A study spot I found

Hi Mia, guess what? I've finally found the perfect place to study, and I had to tell you.

It's a little corner at the back of our local library, right by the window. It's quiet, warm, and best of all free and totally accessible, so I can walk in whenever I like. I love working there.

It's done wonders for my studying. I've been working there every afternoon this week, and being around focused people gives me so much motivation. I even hit a milestone yesterday and finished a whole chapter!

You have to come and try it with me. How about Saturday morning? We could study, then grab lunch. Love, Lena

Greeting — warm hook
The spot + a gerund ("love working")
How it helped — "I've been working…"
Invite + when + sign-off
