The task, in plain words

"Wealthier nations should bear greater responsibility for global problems." Some strongly agree; others think responsibility should be shared. Write an argumentative essay weighing both sides and reaching your own conclusion (120–160 words).

The spine · S-E-R

Situation → Evaluation → Recommendation

Situation
Set the issue neutrally — no side yet. "A difficult question always follows: who should do more?"
Evaluation
Weigh both sides, with hedging. "There is a strong case that…"; "However, the opposing view…"
Recommendation
A measured view. "On balance… shared but differentiated responsibility."
The difference from a one-sided essay: you don't pick a side and defend it — you weigh, then land somewhere fair.

A model essay · watch the S-E-R

"Who Should Do More?"

When the world faces a shared crisis, a difficult question always follows: who should bear the greatest responsibility? Many argue wealthier nations should do more; others insist the burden should be shared.

There is a strong case for asking richer countries to lead. They tend to have the capacity to act quickly, and often produced a disproportionate share of emissions while building their wealth. Accountability, arguably, follows capacity.

However, the opposing view raises fair points. Money alone rarely solves complex problems; without genuine commitment from everyone, even generous funding can be wasted. It would also be unwise to treat developing nations as helpless.

On balance, I would suggest a "shared but differentiated" responsibility: each country contributes according to its means, while richer ones lead. Although fairness demands that those with most do more, lasting solutions will only emerge through mutual effort.
