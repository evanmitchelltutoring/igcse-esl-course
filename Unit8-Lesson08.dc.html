Exam skill · the listening routine

Read → predict → listen → check

You hear each item twice. Use the first play to find the answer and the second to confirm it. Beware the word-match trap.
Read & predict first
Before the audio, read the options and guess the kind of answer each needs. You listen better when you know what you're hunting for.
Don't fall for a word-match
A speaker may say "budget" without agreeing with a view about budgets. Match the meaning, not the word.

Exercise 1 · answers

Main point of each speaker

1. It's the feeling / wonder, not the science.
2. Space is a tiny part of the budget — defence costs more.
3. Everyday satellites came from space tech.
4. Space is only for the rich — doubts "for everyone".
5. The landing was emotional / inspiring, not about the Moon.
6. A failure stopped the programme for two years.
7. Hospital kit traces back to space — not a waste.
8. Moon base first (2030s), Mars only later.

Strongest defenders: 2 (small budget) and 7 (spin-offs); main doubter is 4 (fairness).
