The idea · copy this into your notes

Small & big are not rivals

The lazy view says "one person can't matter". The useful view: individual choices and big systems feed each other. Small actions spread; the people who change their habits are the ones who push leaders to act.
Small choices
One person cuts waste → a friend copies → a canteen changes → hundreds eat differently. It multiplies.
Big systems
Laws and companies decide the most. But the people who vote and campaign for them started by caring.

Which phrase fits?

1. Recycling helps, but it's not enough on its own — we need big changes too.
2. Cutting food waste at school does make a difference, however small.
3. Governments matter most, but we all have a part to play — none of us is off the hook.
4. It starts with one person choosing to reuse, not throw away.
