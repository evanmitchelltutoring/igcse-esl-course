The big idea

A journey changes more than your location — it changes how you see, speak, and belong.

This unit moves from the quick tourist gaze to the slow work of living somewhere new, learning to switch between languages and selves, and carrying a place home with you. The question underneath it all: what does it mean to belong?

Reading in three colours

How we mark up a text

● Meaning
What does it say? The facts and the main point.
● Method
How is it said? Sensory detail, feeling, strong words.
● Response
How do you react? What it reminds you of, or makes you feel.
