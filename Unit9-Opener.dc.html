The big idea

Learning no longer stops at the school gate — the people who thrive are the ones who never stop learning.

This unit looks at how learning really happens: teaching yourself, learning from your mistakes, growing in a changing world, and the mentors and communities that carry us. Your job is to work out how you learn best — and argue for it.
