The big idea

Every morning you get dressed, you answer one quiet question: fit in, or stand out?

This unit looks at what clothes really say — how trends spread, how style expresses the self, what cheap fashion costs the people who make it, and the pull between belonging and being different. Your job is to read the signals, and find your own view.
