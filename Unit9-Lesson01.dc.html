The idea · copy this into your notes

Two ways to see ability

Your mindset is the story you tell yourself about ability. One story stops you learning; the other keeps you going. The whole unit is really about the second one.
Fixed mindset
"I'm just not a maths person." Ability is set; mistakes prove you can't; you avoid hard things.
Growth mindset
"I can't do this yet." Ability grows with effort; mistakes teach; you lean into hard things.
