"a lovely small old wooden table" ✓   not  "a wooden old small lovely table" ✗

You rarely need all five. Two or three, in order, is plenty: a cosy little café.

Grammar · saying where things are

"There is / there are" + place words

is or are?
There is a lamp on the desk. (one)
There are two lamps on the desk. (more than one)
✗ "There is two lamps" — a common slip!
Place words
in the corner on the counter by the window at every seat opposite the door tucked down a side street
Watch me build one

From bare fact to full description

FACT — There is a chair.
+ ADJECTIVES — There is a low, worn wooden chair.
+ PLACE — In the back corner, by the window, there is a low, worn wooden chair.

Practice 1 · put them in order

Reorder the adjectives

a) table / wooden / old / small → a ______________ table
b) café / little / cosy → a ______________ café
c) light / golden / warm → a ______________ light
d) cups / white / chipped → ______________ cups
Stuck? → say each aloud — the natural order is the one that sounds right.
Push further → add a fourth adjective and keep the order correct.
Answers · mark your own

Natural order

a) a small old wooden table
b) a cosy little café
c) a warm golden light
d) chipped white cups

Practice 2 · fill the gaps

Place words — each used once

at by down in on opposite
Tucked (1)___ a side street, the café has a socket (2)___ every seat. A student sits (3)___ the corner, (4)___ the window, and (5)___ the pavement (6)___ an old woman sells flowers.
Answers

Read it back aloud

Tucked down a side street, the café has a socket at every seat. A student sits in the corner, by the window, and on the pavement opposite an old woman sells flowers.

Corrected

1. There are two comfortable chairs by the window.
2. It’s a small wooden table. (size before material)
3. The café is on the corner of the street.
4. There is a quiet room at the back.
5. I sat on a big red sofa. (size before colour)
6. The lamp hangs from the ceiling.
