Before every recording

Predict → Preview → Note

Predict
Guess what you'll hear from the title.
Preview
Read the questions before the audio plays.
Note
Write short answers as you hear them, both times.

Exercise A · complete the notes · 1–3 words
▶ A talk: a wildlife garden

Steps to help wildlife

Leave one corner completely ________
For water, even a ________ will do
Choose plants that flower at ________
Avoid using ________
A pile of dead ________ shelters beetles and hedgehogs
Best time to start: ________
Stuck? → listen for the step words: first, second, third.
Push further → note one extra tip the speaker gives in passing.
Answers · mark your own

Steps to help wildlife

Leave one corner completely wild
For water, even a washing-up bowl will do
Choose plants that flower at different times
Avoid using pesticides
A pile of dead wood shelters beetles and hedgehogs
Best time to start: autumn

Exercise C · choose A, B or C
▶ Interview: a marine biologist

Dr Reyes on ocean plastic

1. When did she realise plastic was a problem?   A in a lab · B while diving, seeing a tangled turtle · C from the news
2. What is the most dangerous plastic?   A floating islands · B plastic bags · C tiny pieces smaller than a grain of rice
3. Who must lead the change?   A individual shoppers · B the companies that make the packaging · C governments
Answers · mark your own

Dr Reyes on ocean plastic

1 · B — she saw a turtle tangled in a bag while diving.
2 · C — the tiny pieces "smaller than a grain of rice".
3 · B — "the real change has to come from the companies".
