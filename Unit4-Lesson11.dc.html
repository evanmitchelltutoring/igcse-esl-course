The task · read it twice
Describe a place that has stayed with you — one that shaped how you see things (a home, a city, a single room, a route you once walked). Write a 150–200 word descriptive piece. Build it Scene → Detail → Verdict; use rich noun phrases and defining relative clauses; let the reader feel the place.

Plan · Scene → Detail → Verdict

Choose your strongest details

Scene
Name the place in one vivid line — "It is the kind of place that…"
Detail
Two or three senses from your Do-Now. Add a relative clause: "…a window that never quite closes."
Verdict
What it shaped in you; the nostalgia or perspective it leaves — "It taught me that…"
Use 5+ target words:   belonging · nostalgia · perspective · enduring · cherish · home

A model description

Read it · then write your own

There is a narrow stairwell at the back of my grandmother's building that I have climbed a thousand times. It is the kind of place you would not look twice at — grey steps, a flickering light, a window that never quite closes. Yet it is the spot where I first felt a real sense of belonging.

I can still smell the rain on its cold concrete, still hear the echo my shoes made on the way up. There was a cracked tile near the top that I learned to step over without thinking. On the landing stood an old chair where my grandmother would wait, a cup of tea cooling beside her.

That stairwell has shaped me more than any grand view could. It taught me that home is not a building but a feeling you carry. Even now, a whiff of damp stone brings back a wave of nostalgia, and with it a quieter perspective. I cherish it still — an ordinary, enduring place I will never forget.
