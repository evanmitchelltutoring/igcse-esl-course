The listening toolkit · copy this

Four moves for every listening part

1 · Read first
Use the seconds before the clip to read the question and options.
2 · Predict
Guess the type of answer — a number? a place? a reason?
3 · Meaning, not words
The answer is usually reworded — listen for what it means.
4 · Second play = check
Answer on the first play; use the second only to confirm.

Station 1 · Part 1 · short recordings

Five clips — choose A, B or C

Each clip is read twice. Read the options before each one.

1 · Water-refill station notice — what must students bring?   A a cup  ·  B their own bottle  ·  C money
2 · Coach, wet pitch — where is football now?   A cancelled  ·  B indoors, in the sports hall  ·  C at a later time
3 · Two friends in a shop — what will the second friend get?   A an energy drink  ·  B a banana and water  ·  C nothing
4 · Student cycling — why did they start?   A to get fit  ·  B to skip the crowded bus  ·  C a parent told them to
5 · Canteen announcement — what replaces the sugary snacks?   A free fruit  ·  B water  ·  C nothing
Predict before you listen: clips 1 & 5 want a thing; clips 2 & 4 want a place/reason. That's move 2.
Answer · mark your own

Station 1 — the answers

1 — B. "bring your own bottle, as we won't be handing out cups."
2 — B. "moving indoors to the sports hall. Same time, just a different place."
3 — B. "I'll just grab a banana and some water."
4 — B. "mainly to skip the crowded bus."
5 — A. "free fruit at break, replacing the sugary snacks."

Station 2 · Part 3 · gapped notes

"Staying energised at school" — one or two words per gap

The talk is read twice. Before you listen, predict: is each gap a food? a time? a number?

Breakfast: choose something slow-release, e.g. ______
Water: finish a bottle by ______; mild dehydration often feels like ______
Movement: even a ______ walk between lessons wakes the brain
Screens: when revising, look away every ______ minutes
Sleep: aim for around ______ hours
Stuck? → the gaps follow the talk's order — one topic at a time.
Push further → which gap is a number? Predicting that makes it easy to catch.
Answer · mark your own

Station 2 — the notes filled

Breakfast: oats / wholegrain toast
Water: by lunchtime; dehydration feels like hunger
Movement: a two-minute walk
Screens: look away every twenty minutes
Sleep: around eight hours

Station 3 — the answers
