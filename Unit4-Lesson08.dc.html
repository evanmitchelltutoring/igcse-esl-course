Teaching · copy this into your notes

Match the pronoun to the noun

who / that
for people — the guide who helped us
which / that
for things — a dish which comes from Japan
where
for places — the city where I was born
drop it!
no repeat: which it comes → which comes
Put the clause straight after the noun it describes: "The market where you can buy spices is busy." The pronoun replaces the repeated word — so you never say "which it" or "where… there".
Watch me first · combine two into one

Drop the repeat, add the pronoun

"This is a tradition. It comes from my region."
↓
This is a tradition which comes from my region.

"which" replaces "it" — the repeated word disappears.

Practice · spot the error

Find and fix one mistake in each

1. A festival is an event which it brings people together.
2. That's the woman which taught me to dance.
3. This is the city where I was born there.
4. A market is a place who you can buy food.
5. Sushi is a dish who comes from Japan.
6. This is the museum which I saw the mask.
Spot the error · answers

Corrected

1. …an event which/that brings people together. (drop "it")
2. …the woman who/that taught me to dance. (people → who)
3. …the city where I was born. (drop "there")
4. …a place where you can buy food. (place → where)
5. …a dish which/that comes from Japan. (thing → which)
6. …the museum where I saw the mask. (place → where)
