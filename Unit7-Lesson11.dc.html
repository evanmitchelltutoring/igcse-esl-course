The frame · copy this into your notes

Develop every point: P-R-R-E

A one-line answer scores low. Stretch each point through four steps and a single idea fills a minute — clearly and without long pauses.
Point
state your claim clearly.
Reason
say why — "because…".
Result
what follows — "which means…".
Example
a real case — "for example…".
show me
Say it before you meet it · repeat after me

Six phrases for justifying & replying

I'd argue that

the key point is

to build on that

if anything

Watch me first · a developed long turn

"A sport I'd defend as skilful"

I'd argue that table tennis is far more skilful than people think. This is because the ball moves so fast that players rely on trained reflexes, not luck. Which means a beginner simply cannot compete with a trained player, for example, my brother has practised for years and I still can't return his serve.

In discussion · concede, then rebut

Answer a counter — don't just contradict it

Flat disagreement ("No, you're wrong") scores low. Grant what's fair, then turn it: "I take your point that gaming isn't tiring, but the reflexes it needs are still physical."
1 · Concede

2 · Rebut
"…but the key point is ______"
3 · Back it
"…because the evidence suggests ______"
