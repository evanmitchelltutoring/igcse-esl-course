The big idea

A mystery is a story with a piece missing — and the skill is reading the clues well.

This unit uses gripping stories to teach real skills: following an investigation, weighing evidence, spotting a hoax, and writing suspense. Your job is to stay curious — and keep your judgement switched on.

The strands of this unit

Four ways to read a mystery

Investigation
Clues, evidence, suspects — how a case is built.
Reasoning
Deduce, conclude, prove — thinking from the facts.
Strangeness
Eerie, baffling, inexplicable — the mood of a mystery.
Hoax or Real?
Reveal, conceal, hoax — testing what to believe.
