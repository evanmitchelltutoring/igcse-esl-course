Grammar · forming the comparison

-er / -est · more / most · irregulars

Short adjectivedry → drier → the driest ·   big → bigger → the biggest
Long adjectivebeautiful → more beautiful → the most beautiful
Irregulargood → better → best · bad → worse → worst · far → further → furthest
Never both: ✗ more bigger · ✗ most driest. Choose one form only.
notebook
Grammar · equal & contrast

Two more ways to compare

Equal — as … as · "The pond is as calm as a mirror."
Less — not as … as · "A pond is not as vast as an ocean."
Contrast — whereas / while · "The desert is dry, whereas the forest is wet."

Entry #3 · Use of English

E
Compare accurately
Short adj: -er/-est. Long adj: more/most. Never both. Use as…as for equal, not as…as for less, and whereas/while to contrast. It's than, not then.
