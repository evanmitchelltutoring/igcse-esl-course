The big idea

Everyone knows what a sport is — until you reach the edges.

Is esports a sport? Is darts? Is chess? To answer, you have to say what a sport really needs — and weigh the money, the crowds and the bodies behind it. This unit gives you the language and the arguments. Your job is to weigh them, and decide what you think.
