Grammar · the passive

be + past participle

ACTIVE
Volunteers planted reeds.
PASSIVE
Reeds were planted (by volunteers).
Use the passive when the action or result matters more than who did it — or when the doer is unknown, obvious, or unimportant. The "by…" part is optional.

Report objectively — the passive
