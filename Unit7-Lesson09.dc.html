Decode the task · before you write

The student council surveyed students about a competitive esports club.

Write a report for the head teacher presenting the survey findings and giving a recommendation. Formal, impersonal. 150–200 words.
1 · Purpose & source
Say what the report is for and where the facts came from.
2 · Findings
Interest, benefits and concerns — under headings, neutrally.
3 · Analysis & recommendation
Say what the findings mean, then recommend a course of action.

The genre · a report presents, it doesn't plead

Sound impersonal and measured

✗ Chatty / personal
"Honestly, everyone I asked really wants this club, so I reckon we should just do it!"
✓ Report register
"The findings indicate strong demand: 68% of those surveyed said they would join or support the club."
Report signals: The purpose of this report is… · The findings indicate… · A significant number… · It is therefore recommended that… Add detail with a relative clause: "the club, which would meet twice a week, …"

A model report · watch the sections

Report: Proposed Esports Club

Introduction. The purpose of this report is to present the findings of a survey of 80 students on a proposed competitive esports club, and to recommend a course of action.

Interest. The findings indicate strong demand: 68% said they would join or support the club, which suggests a genuine following rather than passing curiosity.

Benefits and concerns. Students identified teamwork and quick decision-making as benefits. A significant minority, however, raised concerns about screen time and cost.

Recommendation. The interest is well supported and meets the school's criteria for a new activity. It is therefore recommended that the club be trialled for one term, with a clear budget and screen-time guidance. Such recognition would give it the same legitimate standing as other school teams.
