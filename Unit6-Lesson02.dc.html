The strategy · copy this into your notes

Learn words in families, not alone

One root word usually grows into several. Learn the whole family and you learn four words for the price of one — and you'll always have the right form for a sentence.
One root, four forms
verb  express
noun  expression
adjective  expressive
adverb  expressively
Two ways to fix a word
Chunk it. Store words with their partners: follow a trend, fast fashion.
Use it. A word you say in a real sentence sticks; a word you only read fades.
