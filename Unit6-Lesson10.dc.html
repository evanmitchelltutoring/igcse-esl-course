The genre · report it, don't transcript it

Retell their words in your voice

✗ Transcript
Me: "Why do you wear black?"
Maya: "Because it's simple."
Me: "Do you like colour?"
Maya: "Not really."
✓ Magazine write-up
Maya dresses almost entirely in black. When I asked why, she told me it kept mornings simple — one less decision. She said colour felt like effort she'd rather spend elsewhere.
Reporting verbs: she told me · he said · she explained · he admitted · she added — and always backshift (wear → wore, is → was).

A model write-up · watch the three beats

"Style Stories: Meet Dani"

You can spot Dani from across the yard. While the rest of us blur into the same hoodies, he is wearing a mustard-yellow coat and trainers he has drawn on himself — a signature look that is impossible to miss.

When I asked why he dresses so boldly, he told me he had not always. He explained that he used to dress to disappear, until he realised his clothes could be a kind of self-expression. He said the drawn-on shoes started as a way to cover a stain, and simply grew from there.

What surprised me was how little he cares whether people approve. For Dani, getting dressed is not about being bold for its own sake; it is quiet confidence — proof that he no longer needs to hide.
