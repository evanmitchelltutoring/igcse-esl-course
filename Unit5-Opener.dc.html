Unit 5 · Unit Opener

Art & AI

A machine can make a picture in seconds. But can it make art?

12 lessons Reading · Writing · Listening · Speaking · Use of English
The big idea

When a machine can copy any style in seconds, the question becomes: what is art actually for?

This unit weighs a genuine, live debate — from how AI images are made, to whose work they are trained on, to whether they belong in a gallery. There are strong arguments on every side. Your job is to weigh them, and decide what you think.

The four strands of this unit

Four questions at the heart of the debate

Made by a Machine?
How AI images are actually made — from prompt to output.
Whose Hand, Whose Eye?
What a human brings — intention, emotion, meaning — that a machine may not.
The Style Thief
Whose work AI learns from — consent, ownership and originality.
Real Art, Real Prize?
Should AI work win prizes, hang in galleries, count as art at all?
