Exam skill · the listening routine

Read → predict → meaning → check

Read
the question & options before the clip.
Predict
what a speaker might say for each.
Meaning
listen for the idea, not the exact word.
Check
use the second play to confirm.
The trap: a wrong option often repeats a word you just heard. The right one usually says the same idea in different words.

Exercise 1 · answers

Main point of each speaker

1. Training is relentless — cut if you stop improving.
2. Won over by watching her daughter compete.
3. A gaming final beat basketball for live viewers.
4. A sport should make the body really work.
5. Careers are very short — 24 is old.
6. The crowd / atmosphere gave goosebumps.
7. A real governing structure is in place.
8. Real injuries (wrists) end careers.

Strongest "sport" support: 7 (structure) and 8 (real injury); the main doubter is 4.

Exercise 3 · Part 3 · complete the notes

One or two words per gap

What a sports scientist measures:
• players' reaction times are faster than the __(1)__ adult's
• they make several hundred precise __(2)__ a minute
• the most common injury is __(3)__ in the wrists
• sitting badly causes __(4)__ trouble
• they treat the hands __(5)__ they fail, never after
• they manage __(6)__ and nutrition carefully
Stretch → why does the scientist say "here, slow means losing"?
Exercise 3 · answers

Completed notes

• faster than the average (1) adult's
• several hundred precise movements (2) a minute
• the most common injury is repetitive strain (3) in the wrists
• sitting badly causes back and neck (4) trouble
• they treat the hands before (5) they fail
• they manage sleep (6) and nutrition carefully

Exercise 5 · Part 5 · interview · choose A, B or C

Maya, a federation leader

1. How does Maya feel about Olympic recognition?   A certain it will happen   B realistic — she won't celebrate early   C sure it never will   → __
2. To traditionalists who say gaming doesn't belong, she…   A points to skateboarding & climbing, which faced the same doubt   B dismisses them   C agrees fully   → __
3. On physical exertion, she says it is…   A proof gaming is a sport   B irrelevant   C the strongest argument against, but one test among several   → __
4. If recognition came, the biggest change would be…   A less than expected at the top   B huge at the top   C nothing at all   → __
5. If it doesn't happen in her lifetime, she'll…   A feel she helped lay the track for the next generation   B give up   C feel bitter   → __
Support → listen for where she concedes a point before answering it.
Stretch → where does Maya use the "line has always moved" argument from Lesson 3's backup text?
Exercise 5 · answers

Maya, a federation leader

1 → B realistic — "I've learned not to celebrate before the finish line."
2 → A skateboarding and climbing faced the same scepticism.
3 → C the strongest argument against, but one test among several.
4 → A less would change at the very top; the difference is lower down.
5 → A she'd feel she helped lay the track for whoever comes next.

Notice Q2 and Q3: Maya concedes ("I understand the instinct", "I won't dismiss it") before rebutting — the essay's key move.
