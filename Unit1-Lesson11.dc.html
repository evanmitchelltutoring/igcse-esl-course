Writing · our argument structure

P · E · E

Point
State your claim clearly. What do you think?
Evidence
Give a reason or example — often from a text we read.
Explain
Say why it matters, and link back to the question.
A model argument · P-E-E
Point Evidence Explain

Should towns provide free places for teenagers?

Towns should provide free places for teenagers to gather. In "The Corner That Closed", when the cheap arcade shut, Arif and his friends had "nowhere obvious to go", and the new café sold coffees "none of them can really afford". This matters because without free places, teenagers with little money are quietly excluded from their own town — and a place that welcomes everyone is healthier for the whole community.
