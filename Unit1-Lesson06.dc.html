Way In · why listening is harder

When you read, the words wait for you. When you listen, they vanish.

You can't go back and re-hear a sentence. So the smart listener does the work before the audio starts — and that's exactly what we'll practise.

Answer from your notes

Detail — and one about feeling

1. Why can Maya stay at the library for a long time?
2. What does Maya not have to do to belong there?
3. How much does meeting at the store cost Daniel and his friends?
4. How does Daniel feel about the owner's sign — and why?
5. What word does the presenter use for both their places?
Stuck? → listen for the tone, not just the words.
Push further → note the exact phrase that reveals the feeling.
Support → Find the feeling word first, then its cause. Start: “Maya feels ___ because ___.”
Stretch → Which word gives the feeling away?
