✗ Essay voice

"Fellow students — in a few weeks, you will vote. So ask yourself: what is this prize really for?"

A model speech · watch the five beats

"Keep our art prize human"

Fellow students, teachers — in a few weeks, you will vote on a simple question with a not-so-simple answer: should our art prize accept AI entries? I believe it should not — and let me tell you why.

There is no denying that AI can produce striking images. Some of you will say that if the result is beautiful, it deserves a place, and I understand the appeal. A machine can imitate any style in seconds.

But ask yourself this: what is this prize actually for? It rewards the hours a young artist spends learning, failing, and trying again. A program that borrows a thousand styles overnight has earned none of that — handing it the prize would strip the award of its credibility.

It would rob a real student of the recognition they earned through genuine merit.

So when the vote comes, keep this prize a legitimate celebration of human effort. Keep it for the hands that drew it. Vote to keep our art prize human.
