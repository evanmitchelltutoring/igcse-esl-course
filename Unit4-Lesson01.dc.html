The idea · copy this into your notes

Belonging is built over time

A place moves from strange to yours along a line — and what moves it isn't sightseeing, but time, routine, and the people you come to know.
visitor→ guest→ regular→ belonging
Time
the same streets, seasons and faces, again and again
Routine
knowing where things are and how the day flows
People
being known — and knowing others in return

Say it before you meet it · repeat after me

Six phrases for talking about belonging

a sense of belonging
the feeling that you fit and are accepted
to feel at home
to feel comfortable and relaxed somewhere
out of place
not fitting in; uncomfortable in a setting
to settle into
to grow comfortable in a new place or situation
to adapt to
to change so you fit a new situation
over time
gradually, across weeks and months
