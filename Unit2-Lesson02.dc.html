Stuck? → read the whole sentence first, then choose the word.
Push further → cover the bank and try from memory.
Answers · mark your own

The completed passage

Our planet is home to millions of species of plants and animals, each living in its own habitat. Some places are lush, green and full of life; others are barren and lifeless after a long drought. Many animals navigate huge distances each year, guided by instinct, simply to survive. But human pollution now threatens many habitats, and some species become endangered. Through conservation, people work to protect and preserve the natural world.
